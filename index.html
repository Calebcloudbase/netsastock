🏢 About NetsaStock

NetsaStock is a specialized warehouse system built for automotive spare parts management — engineered for store managers and family-operated businesses who need clean tracking, card grid layouts, and accurate revenue metrics.

⚡ Core Application Features

  • 🎯 Dual-Main Divisions: Segmented into BABAJ (TVS King / Hora RE) and BOXER.
  • ⚠️ Critical Stock Alerts: Dedicated panel for low stock items, sorted by lowest quantity.
  • 💵 Financial Capital Engine: Instant valuation of total locked funds (Quantity × Buying Price).
  • 📈 Profit Ledger: Auto-calculates net margins per sale, with return tracking.

📖 Application User Manual

1. Managing Inventory

Click 📦 Stock. Filter by TVS King, Hora RE, or Boxer. Sell, Restock, Edit, or Delete items using card controls.

2. Adding New Spare Parts

Click ➕ Add Item, enter details, and Save. Form inputs reset automatically on save.

3. Selling & Returns

Click Sell on any stock card. To process a return, navigate to Analytics → Today and click Return.
